## 3. Hard cases and judgment calls

1. **The held invoice does get a filing number (05 → 04826).** Martha says "no la mando a ningún lado" (I don't send it anywhere), but the fraud attempt must be traceable in the ledger that Accounting and Legal consult. It is filed with status `ON_HOLD`, department Coordination, not forwarded for payment; the outbox produces a fraud alert for Dra. Herrera. The document is in REVIEW and a suspected fraud is **never approved**: Martha alerts Dra. Herrera with "Alertar a la Dra. Herrera" (`POST /api/inbox/{id}/escalate`, audit `HOLD_ESCALATED`); trying to approve it answers `409 HOLD_REQUIRES_ESCALATION`, and "Aprobar listos" skips it even if it was corrected to READY. It does not end there: the invoice appears in Dra. Herrera's "Por verificar" queue (`/verifications`), next to the supplier master, and after her phone call she releases it or confirms the fraud, so "Retenida" is no longer a dead end.
2. **The historical ledger already contains a duplicate.** Sabana Express invoice `X-10504` appears twice (TAC-2026-ER-04809 and 04813): exactly the problem Martha describes. Docket does not modify history; it reports it via `GET /api/ledger/duplicates` (`test_excel_ledger.py::test_historical_duplicates_are_reported`).
3. **Real duplicate in the mailbox (06, FE-1207).** No new number: the proposal is to reply with `TAC-2026-ER-04790`.
4. **Travel request approved by the coordinator (09).** Diana Ortiz's form is approved by Dra. Claudia, not by someone titled "boss": accepted as a valid line-manager approval. It is filed, flagged late (departs 19-Nov, fewer than 5 business days).
5. **Petition asking only for copies (12) → 10 days.** A journalist asks for copies of documents and nothing else, so the 10-day term applies. Copies plus something else would be 15 (the lawyer's rule).
6. **Implicit petitions.** Texts with "solicito", "les pido que me informen" that are not complaints from terminal users are treated as petitions; the `PETITION_IMPLICIT` reason makes it visible for Martha to confirm.
7. **Oversight notice (11).** The term is the notice's (5 business days → 2026-11-23), not the statutory one, and it is urgent. If the notice has no readable term, it goes to review instead of assuming one.
8. **Holidays.** 16-Nov (holiday) pushes petition 10 to 2-Dec; 8-Dec pushes complaint 14 to 9-Dec. Holidays inside the term are shown as `HOLIDAY_IN_TERM`.
9. **Numbering is assigned on approval, not on import.** A discarded or under-review document leaves no gaps, and order follows e-mail reception. Filing numbers are never reused.
10. **Scan without OCR.** If Tesseract is not installed, the document is not guessed: `DOCUMENT_UNREADABLE`, and the API refuses to file it without a department (`test_approve_and_correct.py::test_unreadable_document_cannot_be_filed_without_a_department`).
11. **Where a released invoice goes.** Martha did not say; Docket sends it where it would have gone without the hold (her purchase-order rule: with PO → Accounts payable, without → Procurement), so invoice 05 (OC-2026-0377) goes to Accounts payable, with a reminder to pay only the master's account. A confirmed fraud is never paid: the ledger row reads "Rechazada - fraude confirmado" and both Accounts payable and Procurement are warned. Only the coordinator resolves, with a written note, and each resolution is audited (`HOLD_RELEASED` / `HOLD_REJECTED`).
